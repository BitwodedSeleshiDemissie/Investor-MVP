How to Use This

Share this screen and go row by row. The question is simple: is this the correct way to calculate and display this metric from the Excel file?

  • Is the formula accurate?
  • Are we pulling from the right Excel field?
  • Should the dashboard calculate it, or read it directly from Excel?
  • Anything here that should be renamed or handled differently?
